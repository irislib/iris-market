What you can do

Listings

Browse offers and requests as a list, photo grid or map.

Search

Search by keyword, or describe what you want and let AI on your device find it.

Messages

Chat privately with sellers in encrypted messages.

Pickup and delivery

Agree on pickup, book a courier or request a ride.

Bitcoin offers

Search “bitcoin” to find people buying or selling it.

Wallet

Send and receive bitcoin with the built-in Cashu wallet.

Escrow

Let someone you both trust settle a trade. Two of you must agree.

Private matching

Keep an offer or request off the public market. A helper you trust finds matches, and the other person sees only a summary until you both choose to connect.

Command line

Search listings, post offers and requests, and read messages from the terminal. Try it offline with sample data, or connect an AI assistant through MCP.

CLI and MCP reference ↗

Windows CLI requires the Microsoft Visual C++ x64 runtime.

cargo install iris-market --version 0.1.4 --locked
iris-market --help
iris-market tools
iris-market preview ./preview-data
